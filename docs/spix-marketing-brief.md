# Spix marketing brief

*For a launch campaign. Written 29 October 2026 from the agreed offer ("What Spix Sells", 17 Oct), the launch rules
in `docs/rework-tracker.md` (D36–D38) and the brand in `docs/brand/`. Prices, tiers and the launch date are not
decided yet; fill them in before anything goes out.*

---

## 1. The idea in one line

**Know the real odds. Never overpay.**

Every bet has a fair price. Bookmakers build their margin into the odds, so most bettors pay more than a bet is
worth without knowing it. Spix shows the real chance and the fair price for every match, so bettors can see in
seconds whether their bookmaker is offering a good price or a bad one.

## 2. Who we're talking to

**Main audience: the matchday bettor.** 18+, follows football closely, bets most weekends on their phone, mostly
singles, bet builders and small accumulators. They:
- know football, but not how odds are made;
- suspect the bookmaker always wins, and hate feeling taken for a ride;
- are tired of tipsters with screenshots of winning slips and "95% hit rate" claims;
- want to feel smart about their bets, not be told what to do.

**Second audience: the careful bettor.** Already compares prices, has heard of "value" and "closing line value",
and wants a tool that does the maths faster and more honestly than a spreadsheet.

**Not our audience:** people looking for guaranteed wins, people chasing losses, anyone under 18. We don't write
for them, and our ads must not appeal to them.

## 3. What Spix does (in the bettor's words)

| What they get | What it means for them |
|---|---|
| **The real chance** of each outcome, with the bookmakers' margin taken out | "Now I know what this bet is actually worth." |
| **The fair price, and the lowest price worth taking** (fair odds plus 2%) | "I can check my app and know in seconds whether it's a good price." |
| **Green cards and red cards** on the Daily Card | "Here's what's worth a look today, and what to skip." |
| **The Match Read**: the research for each match done for them (form, goals, corners, cards, head to head) | "I don't have to spend an hour on stats sites." |
| **Price Check**: check any single, bet builder or boost | "Is this boost actually a boost?" |
| **The Ledger**: a personal record of their bets | "I can finally see how much I've been overpaying." |
| **A public record** of every pick, wins and losses | "They don't hide the bad weeks." |

Coverage: the top five European leagues, plus the Championship, Eredivisie, Primeira Liga, Belgian Pro League,
Süper Lig, and the Champions, Europa and Conference Leagues.

## 4. Why Spix, and not a tipster

| Typical tipster | Spix |
|---|---|
| Sells a win rate | Sells knowledge of the real price |
| Shows winning slips, hides the rest | Publishes every pick, wins and losses |
| "Trust me, back this" | "Here's the fair price: decide for yourself" |
| Often paid by bookmakers per sign-up | No affiliate deals: we earn nothing when you lose |
| Promises profit | Promises honesty, and says plainly that nobody can guarantee profit |

**Our proof is the method and the record, never a profit figure.** Spix judges itself every week against the
closing price, the toughest yardstick in betting, and publishes the result.

## 5. Key messages

1. **The odds are only half the story.** Spix shows you the other half: what the bet is really worth.
2. **Never overpay.** Know the lowest price worth taking before you open your bookmaker's app.
3. **Skip the bad bets.** The red cards are as useful as the green ones.
4. **Your research, done.** One Match Read covers what used to take an hour across five sites.
5. **Honest by design.** No affiliate money, no win-rate claims, every pick on the public record.

## 6. Brand voice

Our brand words are **playful, trustworthy, sharp, honest, calm**.

- Talk like a friend who knows the numbers: plain words, short sentences, a little wit.
- Confident, never hype. No "LOCK", no "GUARANTEED", no rockets, no money-bag emojis.
- Explain, don't preach. A tone of "here's the price, your call".
- Football first: matchday language, real fixtures, real moments.

**Sounds like Spix:** "Brighton +1 is worth 1.94. Your app says 1.88? Skip it."
**Doesn't sound like Spix:** "🔥 MASSIVE VALUE BET 🔥 Don't miss out, 10/10 confidence!!!"

## 8. Campaign ideas

**1. "What's it really worth?" (hero series, social video and carousels)**
Take one big fixture each week. Show the odds at the bookmakers, then reveal Spix's fair price, and point out which
price is a good deal and which isn't. The ball from the logo drops onto the "i" as the reveal. Repeatable every
matchday.

**2. "The boost check" (short-form, weekly)**
Pick a heavily advertised "enhanced odds" offer and run it through Price Check on camera. Sometimes it's genuinely
good, and we say so. Honest either way is the point, and it's the most shareable format.

**3. "Red card of the week" (social)**
The most popular bet of the weekend that Spix says to skip, and why, in two lines. Shows the value of the red cards.

**4. "The open book" (trust)**
A monthly post of the public record, the bad weeks included, with a plain explanation. Rare in this category,
and the strongest trust signal we have.

**5. Discord community (retention and word of mouth)**
A free channel with one Match Read a day and the public record; members get the full Daily Card. The Discord
avatar is the full "spix" logo on turf green.

**6. Creator partnerships (later, with care)**
Football analysts and data creators, not "tipsters". Paid partnerships must be labelled as ads, and creators may
not make profit or win-rate claims on our behalf.

## 10. Rules we never break (legal and brand)

These come from the launch decisions and gambling advertising rules. Check every asset against them before it goes
out.

- **No promise of profit or winnings.** No hit rates, no "% ROI", no screenshots of winning slips, no "beat the
  bookies".
- **Never suggest betting is a way to make money,** solve money problems or improve your life.
- **18+ only.** Nothing that appeals to under-18s (no youth culture, cartoons aimed at kids, or young-looking
  people in assets).
- **Responsible-gambling message and help line on every ad,** local to the country (e.g. BeGambleAware in the UK).
- **Name only bookmakers licensed in the viewer's country.** No affiliate links or deals.
- **Label paid partnerships as ads.**
- **No paid ads aimed at Italy** (advertising ban). **UK:** follow the ASA/CAP rules for tipster services.
- **Romania:** check the ONJN reply and the pending 06:00–24:00 advertising bill before any Romanian campaign.
- **Prices shown in ads must be real and dated** ("checked at 14:05"), never invented.
- **Don't use club badges, player images or league logos without rights.** Use team names and our own graphics.
