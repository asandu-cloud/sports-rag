Privacy notice — draft

Draft for local review. This is not a completed privacy notice. The operator’s legal identity, contact details and data-handling terms still need confirmation. Paid sign-up is not offered on the landing page.

Information used by the current website

If you choose to create or sign in to an account, the application supports email/password and Discord sign-in. Account records can include an email address, a password hash or Discord identifier, display name and avatar, and account status. Subscription and referral records are supported where those features are used.

The browser stores a sign-in token in local storage to keep you signed in. Logging out removes that token from the browser. It does not delete the account record.

External services

Fonts are served from this website. The website may load football imagery from API-Sports; those requests expose connection information to API-Sports. Discord sign-in and community links involve Discord. Stripe is the payment integration in the application; paid sign-up is not currently offered on this landing page.

What still needs to be completed

The final notice must identify the operator and privacy contact and explain each processing purpose and lawful basis, recipients, relevant international transfers, retention periods or criteria, and how to exercise data rights. Those details have not been confirmed and are not invented here.

Learn about your rights or raise a data-protection concern through the Information Commissioner’s Office. The ICO’s privacy-information guidance informs the completion checklist.
