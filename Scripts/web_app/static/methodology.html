How to read the analysis

Spix brings football forecasts and potential betting selections into a fixture-level Match Read. The numbers describe an uncertain outlook; they do not guarantee an outcome or a return.

From match history to a forecast

Historical team production, opponent concessions, recent form and venue context inform projected team and match totals. Goals, corners, cards and shots on target are count forecasts. Both teams to score, match result and handicap markets describe other possible outcomes.

The statistical engine is the current foundation. Research models and experimental player adjustments are evaluated separately; an experiment is not proof of a feature being active.

A projection is not a probability

A total-goals projection of 2.8 is an average across possible outcomes. It does not mean the match will finish with three goals. Probability distributions, including Poisson-family approaches, translate projected rates into chances for individual outcomes. Distribution assumptions and missing data affect those estimates.

A probability is not a recommendation

A price changes the decision. Quotes come through API-Football and may have moved or be unavailable to you. Check the bookmaker, side, line, period, timestamp and settlement rules. Cards and handicaps especially require an exact market match.

Confidence labels describe the system’s assessment; they are not guarantees or independently established win rates. A positive estimated edge alone does not demonstrate profitable betting. Spix does not claim a verified profit record or universally calibrated probabilities.

What a Match Read tells you

Read the fixture outlook and its limitations alongside each selection. There may be up to three compatible selections, or no bet. The headline shortlist contains at most five fixtures. Same-game selections can be dependent; multiplying their individual probabilities is not a reliable estimate of their combined chance.

Published reads can change as new information arrives. A fixture marked “Analysis pending” has no published Match Read yet. Schedule coverage does not mean every league, fixture or market has equally strong evidence.

Understand edge and expected value

This calculator is an educational example for a single win-or-lose market with no push, void or commission. You supply the probability; the calculator does not validate it or select a bet.

Expected profit per £1 = (probability × decimal odds) − 1. An estimate of £0.08 does not promise an 8p return on any individual bet. Whole-stake losses remain possible.
