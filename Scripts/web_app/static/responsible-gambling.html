Keep gambling in perspective

Spix is for adults aged 18 and over. Betting should never be treated as a source of income or a way to solve money problems. A confident-looking prediction can still lose.

Give yourself room to stop

  • Set a money and time limit before you start, and stick to it.
  • Only use money you can afford to lose. Do not borrow to bet or chase losses.
  • Take breaks. Passing on a match is always an option.
  • If gambling is affecting your money, relationships or wellbeing, seek support.

Free, confidential UK support

GamCare offers support through the National Gambling Helpline, available 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Call 0808 8020 133 or use the support options on its website.

You can also read the NHS guidance on help for gambling problems.

A forecast is not a promise

Prices change, information can be missing and models make mistakes. An estimated edge does not establish that you will make money. You do not need to place a bet to follow football or use the analysis.
