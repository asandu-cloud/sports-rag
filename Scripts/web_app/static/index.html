Frequently asked questions

We use a combination of Poisson and Negative Binomial distributions for count-based markets (goals, corners, cards, shots on target), Elo ratings for team strength, and Ridge regression models trained on 1,800+ fixtures across 8 leagues. Each projection blends season averages (90%) with recent form (10%) and venue adjustments.

Value edge is the difference between our model's probability and the bookmaker's implied probability. If we calculate Over 2.5 Goals at 62% but the bookie prices it at 53%, that is a +8.5% value edge - meaning the bet is priced in your favor.

We cover 5 domestic leagues (EPL, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1) plus 3 European competitions (UCL, UEL, UECL). Markets include: Moneyline, Goals Totals, Corners, Cards, Shots on Target, BTTS, Handicap/Spreads, Correct Score, and Goal Intervals.
