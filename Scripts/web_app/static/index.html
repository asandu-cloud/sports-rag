A few things to know.

What is a Match Read?

A fixture assessment that brings the match outlook, reasoning and available selections together. A read can contain up to three compatible selections, or no bet. Same-game selections are related; they are not independent chances of winning.

Will every fixture have a recommendation?

No. The schedule includes fixtures awaiting analysis. A published read may also contain no bet, and the headline shortlist contains at most five fixtures. Availability depends on the data, market and price.

Can I rely on the price shown?

A displayed quote is a recorded price, not a guarantee that you can place a bet at that price. Check its timestamp, the current bookmaker price and the exact market rules. If the price is unavailable or unsuitable, pass.

Does Spix guarantee a profit?

No. Predictions can be wrong and you can lose money. A model probability, confidence label or estimated edge is not proof of profitability. Only bet what you can afford to lose. Find gambling support.
