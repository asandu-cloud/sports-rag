Straight answers

Does Spix guarantee a profit?
No. Predictions can be wrong and you can lose money. Only bet what you can afford to lose.
Does Spix tell me what to bet?
It recommends. You choose what goes on your bet, and you can change anything or add your own ideas.
Are these AI predictions?
Not the chatbot kind. Spix's chances come from statistical models, thousands of past matches and bookmakers' prices, and every pick is measured after the match. Machine learning only gets a say once it proves better in testing. A language model helps word some match summaries, using only those figures. It doesn't set the chances or choose the picks.
Why is the price "typical"?
It's the middle of the prices across bookmakers. Yours may differ a little, so check the real price in your own app before you bet.
Can I place a bet on Spix?
No. Spix gives analysis and doesn't take bets. You place your bet with your own bookmaker.
What if betting stops being fun?
Stop, and talk to someone. Our responsible gambling page explains where to find help.
